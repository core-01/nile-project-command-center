# Business Requirements

## Customer and commercial
- Support B2B and B2C customer records.
- Capture enquiries, quotations, pricing, contracts and bookings.
- Convert confirmed bookings into transport orders.

## Operations
- Manage shipments, trips, vehicles, drivers, pickup and delivery events.
- Capture proof of delivery and trip expenses.
- Support owned and attached fleet allocation.

## Finance
- Generate invoices, record receipts, manage receivables and payables.
- Settle partners and calculate trip/vehicle profitability.
- Introduce accounting controls before financial posting.

## Platform
- Organization and branch management, users, roles and permissions.
- Tenant isolation must be designed and tested before SaaS onboarding.
- Audit sensitive business and financial actions.

Requirements are initial planning scope and need business validation before implementation.
