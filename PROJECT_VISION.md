# Project Vision

Nile Technologies intends to establish a freight transportation business and build a logistics ERP / Transportation Management System that can later become a multi-tenant SaaS product.

Initial planning assumption: approximately 50 vehicles, combining owned and attached capacity. This is a planning assumption, not an operational fleet count.

## Outcomes
- Manage customer demand, quotations, contracts and bookings.
- Coordinate vehicles, drivers, shipments and trips.
- Control trip costs, invoicing, settlements and profitability.
- Provide operational visibility and later fleet intelligence.
- Establish a SaaS-ready platform after the business MVP.

## Project boundary
The Project Command Center is an internal static engineering workspace. It is not the logistics application itself.
