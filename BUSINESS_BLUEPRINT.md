# Business Blueprint

## Operating model
Freight transportation using company-owned commercial vehicles and third-party attached vehicles. Target customers include B2B organizations and B2C individuals.

## Fleet models
### Owned fleet
Purchase/financing, insurance, driver allocation, fuel, maintenance, depreciation and vehicle profitability.
### Attached fleet
Partner registration, KYC, contracts, vehicle verification, trip allocation, settlement, commission and margin.

## Revenue models
Per trip, per kilometre, dedicated vehicle contracts, monthly transportation contracts and attached-vehicle margin. Additional logistics services are future scope.

## Cost drivers
Fuel, driver salaries and allowances, toll, maintenance, insurance, EMI, depreciation, partner payments, employee and administrative expenses.

## Lifecycle
Lead → Customer → Quotation → Contract → Booking → Order → Vehicle/Driver allocation → Trip → Delivery confirmation → Invoice → Payment → Profitability.
